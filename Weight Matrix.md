## Design 1

|                                                                | Weight | Score | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Clarity of Differential Demonstration**                      | 0.20   | 4     | The transparent transfer case and visible axle differentials (Features #1 and #4) allow students to directly observe how wheel speeds differ during turns. This makes the internal mechanical action highly intuitive.           |
| **Clarity of Transfer Case Demonstration**                     | 0.20   | 4     | The transparent transfer case (Feature #2) combined with the selector lever (Feature #3) clearly demonstrates the 2WD to 4WD shift mechanism. Students can visually confirm the mechanical engagement.                           |
| **Lab usability (setup + test time, all 4 students involved)** | 0.15   | 3     | Modular road sections (Feature #8) and adjustable incline (Feature #5) allow for quick reconfiguration of test scenarios. However, setting up different obstacle courses might take extra time during a lab session.             |
| **Safety**                                                     | 0.14   | 4     | Uses electric motor power (Concept) with no exposed high-speed rotating parts (fully enclosed under the clear case), and the adjustable incline has secure height-adjustment screws, minimizing pinch hazards.                   |
| **Serviceability (Ease of repair)**                            | 0.10   | 4     | The modular design of the road surface (Feature #8) and the accessible placement of the axle differential and steering tie rod make replacing broken parts straightforward without dismantling the whole structure.              |
| **Ease of storage/transport**<br>                              | 0.08   | 3     | As noted, the modular road sections (Feature #8) can be disassembled for storage. However, the overall system still involves many separate components and support structures, requiring careful packing.                         |
| **Cost margin below $300**                                     | 0.06   | 2     | The requirement for multiple transparent components (transfer case), adjustable mechanisms (height screws/ramp), and modular track pieces likely drives material and manufacturing costs higher than a simple static model.      |
| **Easy to manufacture**                                        | 0.05   | 3     | While the vehicle chassis and steering components are relatively standard, fabricating the adjustable incline ramp and ensuring precise alignment of the modular road joints requires moderate manufacturing complexity.         |
| **Future Proof (Ease of future additions)**                    | 0.02   | 5     | The modular road system (Feature #8) is highly expandable. Future additions such as uneven surfaces or new terrain modules can be easily integrated, and the vehicle's open chassis allows for adding new sensors or mechanisms. |
| **Total**                                                      | 1      | 3.62  |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
