# Candidate Design 3

**Design context:** CD3 is a hand-cranked stationary drivetrain demonstrator with suspended wheels and no steering mechanism or road course.

**Specification source:** [[Engineering-Specifications/Engineering Specification v1|Engineering Specification v1]] — §1.2 Project Scope and all 18 detailed requirements in §2. The English requirement wording and targets below are transcribed from that version. The rationale and evidence columns describe the concept and the checks needed to verify compliance.

A fixed-base different-speed demonstration does not by itself establish the curved-path, full-time/part-time turning, or locked-axle turning criteria. Explain the proposed method and evidence for these criteria before assigning a result.

## §2 — Demonstration

| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **D1 — Differential Function**       | **Description:** **Must** allow differential action to be demonstrated at the front, rear, and centre positions of the drivetrain.<br>**Target:** Differential behaviour shall be demonstrated at all three positions.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | The front, rear and centre differentials are intended to demonstrate differential action visible through transparent covers.                                | Verify and demonstrate differential action at each of the three positions.                                                                                            |
| **D2 — Observable Speed Difference** | **Description:** **Must** allow students to deliberately create and visually distinguish a different-speed condition between the outputs.<br>**Target:** At least one student-controlled different-speed condition shall be available.
